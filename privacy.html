Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Weening ("the app") is developed by SimpleCodingSolutions LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: we don't collect it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to any personal information or usage data from the app. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking tools in the app.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter, including check-ins, hit counts, moods, cravings, journal entries, voice recordings you choose to keep, and your quit plan, is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage. We cannot see it.

Voice journaling

If you use voice journaling, your speech is transcribed using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is not sent to us. Recordings are only kept if you choose to save them, and they stay on your device. The app asks for microphone and speech recognition permission only when you first use this feature, and you can turn it off in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Notifications

Reminders and notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. We do not run a notification server.

Purchases

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or know who made a purchase. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Backups and exports

Your app data may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by you and Apple. If you use "Export my data" or automatic backups, the app saves a file to a location you choose, such as iCloud Drive or your iPhone's Files app. We never receive these files.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. Feedback emails from the app pre-fill your app version, iOS version, and device model to help us fix problems; you can delete these before sending. We use emails only to reply to you, and we'll delete your messages on request. The app never attaches your quitting data, moods, or journal entries.

Links to other services

The app may link to outside services, such as free quitlines, crisis lines, or meditation apps. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

Weening is designed for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time with "Delete all my data" in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app. Because your data is never sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above. If we ever begin collecting any information, we'll update this policy before doing so.
